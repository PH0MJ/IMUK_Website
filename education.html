Academic & Humanitarian Outreach

Medical Education & Clinical Training

Delivering postgraduate surgical simulation, emergency resuscitation workshops with the Red Crescent Society, and accredited continuing professional development.

Humanitarian Mission

Red Crescent Emergency Clinical Workshop

Conducted in partnership with the Iraqi Red Crescent Society, this intensive programme brings British and European NHS trauma specialists to train frontline clinicians in advanced disaster resuscitation, triage management, and emergency surgical stabilization.

  • ✓ Multi-casualty incident management protocols
  • ✓ Ultrasound-guided emergency vascular access
  • ✓ Non-invasive airway and thoracic drainage simulation
Postgraduate Specialisation

Karbala MRCOG / Royal College Exam Masterclass

Designed to bridge Iraqi obstetricians and gynecologists with Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists (RCOG) standards. Senior UK consultants deliver mock OSCE circuits, clinical audit teaching, and evidence-based guideline reviews.

  • ✓ Mock OSCE clinical stations with consultant feedback
  • ✓ Critical appraisal of high-impact obstetrical literature
  • ✓ Safe operative delivery and fetal monitoring interpretation
Virtual Academic Hub

Joint International Webinars & Lectures

Live and recorded lectures accessible to registered members worldwide.

Clinical Solidarity

Joint International Webinars

Organised by Dr. Manal Nasih and UK NHS specialists, providing consultations and expert case reviews for local Iraqi patients with complex conditions.

Royal College Accreditation

RCP-Approved CPD Points

Accredited educational sessions and annual scientific conference symposia earning formal Royal College of Physicians CPD credits for GMC revalidation.

Humanitarian Training

Trauma & Disaster Workshops

Advanced hands-on emergency training delivered in collaboration with the Iraqi Red Crescent Society and regional medical centers in Iraq.
