Humanitarian Collaboration

Red Crescent Clinical Workshops & Training

Our members actively volunteer their expertise, providing surgical training, emergency medicine workshops, and specialized clinical masterclasses in collaboration with the Iraqi Red Crescent Society and European humanitarian partners.

  • Emergency resuscitation and trauma simulation
  • Specialized surgical webinars with live Q&A
  • Direct donation of modern medical equipment & journals

Supporting the Next Generation of Doctors

Moving to the UK or European healthcare systems can be challenging. Our senior fellows provide 1-on-1 guidance every step of the way.

GMC & PLAB Guidance

Preparation tips, OSCE workshops, and structured clinical revision sessions taught by recent successful examinees.

NHS Induction & Job Support

CV review, interview preparation, and understanding NHS clinical culture, electronic health records, and audit portfolios.

Pan-European Recognition

Advice for Iraqi medical graduates practicing or pursuing postgraduate training in Germany, France, Ireland, and Scandinavia.
