Official Charter Privileges

The 7 Core Benefits of Joining the IMA

Every approved member enjoys full access to our academic programs, clinical network, and governance rights.

1

Medical Career Development

Specialized advisory services covering NHS specialty training (ST1–ST8), CESR applications, portfolio building, and consultant interview readiness panels.

2

Peer Collaboration & Clinical Teams

Uniting you with highly qualified colleagues across British and European institutions, providing multi-disciplinary teams (MDT) to work with and resources.

3

Exclusive Member Privileges

Direct member benefits and guaranteed fee waivers or discounts on all IMA scientific symposia, masterclasses, and prestigious annual dinner banquets.

4

RCP-Approved CPD Points

Keeping up to date with recent advances by attending accredited sessions and earning Royal College of Physicians-approved CPD credits for GMC revalidation.

5

International Lectures & Workshops

Participating in live and online lectures in London, Europe, and joint collaborative academic events with Iraq, the Arab world, and global medical societies.

6

Professional Network Expansion

Expanding your clinical network across continental Europe and joining interest-based member sub-committees, clinical audits, and policy initiatives.

7

Trainee Doctor International Bursaries

Access to competitive financial grants and bursaries dedicated to assisting junior doctors and international medical graduates (IMGs) to present original research papers at international conferences.
