Institutional Chronicle

About Us & Our Enduring Governance

Founded in 1991 in London, uniting Iraqi medical consultants, researchers, and trainees across Great Britain and Europe under a democratic, transparent charter.

Historical Foundation

The Founding of the Association (1991)

The Iraqi Medical Association was founded in 1991, following the conclusion of the Second Gulf War and the near-total destruction of infrastructure in our beloved homeland.

The association's primary goal, among other humanitarian and academic objectives, was to unite Iraqi doctors in the United Kingdom and Ireland, who were eager to assist our colleagues and people in Iraq during unprecedented hardships, while fostering an academic and social community abroad.

Constitutional Aims

Our Core Association Objectives

1. Scientific Advancement

Fostering medical research, continuing professional development (CPD), and annual scientific congresses accredited by British Royal Colleges.

2. Professional Solidarity

Providing mentoring for newly arrived international medical graduates (IMGs), trainee bursaries, and peer-to-peer clinical guidance across Europe.

3. Humanitarian Education

Partnering with humanitarian organizations such as the Red Crescent Society to deliver medical supplies, trauma masterclasses, and simulation training.
