The Iraqi Medical Association was founded in 1991, following the end of the Second Gulf War and the near-total destruction of infrastructure in our beloved Iraq. This exacerbated the suffering of our people at home.
The association's primary goal, among other objectives, was to unite Iraqi doctors in the United Kingdom and Ireland, where there was a dire need for scientific and social communication, and to provide assistance to new doctors in securing jobs in British hospitals. We also extended a helping hand to our people inside the country and attempted to break the unjust blockade by all possible means.
The Iraqi Medical Association was headed for the first year by
Dr. Hisham Hassan,
followed by the following doctors, who won the democratic and transparent elections:
Dr. Maher Al-Hilali, Dr. Ferial Ahmed, Dr. Ismail Al-Jalili, Dr. Khaled Al-Shafi, Dr. Walid Al-Wali, Dr. MOHAMAD AL-Sadi, Dr. Nabil Al-Hamami, Dr. Alaa Al-Husseini, Dr. Ahmed Al-Jawad, Dr. Basil Al-Shihabi, Dr. Bahaa Al-Wakil, and Dr. Riad Abid.
The Association succeeded in attracting a large number of Iraqi doctors in Britain from all specialties, with the number of registered members exceeding 700. The quarterly meetings were held at Ealing Hospital in London, with distinguished attendance. We were honored to invite professors from the College of Medicine in Baghdad to deliver various lectures at these meetings. The late
Dr. Farhan Baqir, Dr. Zuhair Al-Bahrani, Ihsan Al-Bahrani, Khaled Naji, Abdul Amir Alawi, Salem Al-Damluji, Abdul Majeed Al-Alusi, Hussein Al-Talib, Alaa Al-Din Al-Khalidi, Sanha, and Laman Amin Zaki attended.
The efforts of the association's members were also crowned with fruitful success in holding the annual meetings, which were attended by a large number of members and others, along with their families. Two distinguished days of scientific lectures and cultural lectures were also held, including lectures on fine arts, music, and literature. It is worth noting that one of the artistic evenings was hosted by the singer Hussein Nimah. In 1999, the association participated in the Iraqi community conference held in Hammersmith, London, during which medical advice was provided to members of the community in various specialties, which was well received by the attendees. The association's activities were not limited to meetings; it also took the initiative to organize a family river trip for members on the tames river.
The association published a quarterly newsletter in Arabic, covering member news and activities, as well as interviews with professors at the Baghdad College of Medicine and general cultural topics.
Over the last few years we had some fascinating local Iraqi members like
Dr Manal Nasih
and
Dr Tahsin Mizhar
who have contributed a lot to the activity of our society. Dr Manal was helping in organising our joint international webinars. Many of them involved consultations with specialists at UK for a group of local Iraqi patients who were having multiple complicated issues. We greatly appreciate all this voluntary work by her which have resulted in solving many complex medical issues.

New members joining the IMA will benefit from the following:
1. Medical career development.
2. Uniting you with your highly qualified medical colleagues and providing you with teams to work with and resources.
3. Getting exclusive member benefits and discounts on all IMA future scientific, cultural and social events.
4. Keeping up to date with recent advances by attending events and earning Royal College of Physicians-approved CPD points.
5. Participating in online and live lectures and workshops in London, UK and Europe, and joint events with Iraq, the Arab world, our North American and Australian IMAs, international symposia and workshops
6. Expanding your professional network and joining interest-based member groups.
7. Bursaries for trainee doctors to join international meetings.

Flash  Disclaimer
The IMA UK & Europe is aware that certain individuals or agents are sending invitations to doctors in Iraq using the name and logo of the IMA UK & Europe in an attempt to obtain UK visas for attendance at our meetings.
Please note that the IMA UK & Europe does not have any agents or representatives inside Iraq, nor have we authorised any individual or organisation to issue invitations on our behalf.
We would like to assure all colleagues that any official activity, conference, or event organised by the IMA UK & Europe will always be advertised on our official website and social media platforms. All invitations will be sent directly by the IMA UK & Europe to Iraqi doctors wishing to participate in our events.
The IMA UK & Europe has already informed the relevant authorities about these fraudulent documents and has requested the rejection of any applications submitted using such fake invitations.
We strongly advise recipients to disregard any invitation received from any other source, whether inside or outside Iraq, unless it has been issued directly by the IMA UK & Europe.

تود الجمعية الطبية العراقية في المملكة المتحدة وأوروبا (IMA UK & Europe)
أن تبين بأن بعض الأشخاص أو الجهات يقومون بإرسال دعوات إلى الأطباء في العراق مستخدمين اسم وشعار الجمعية، في محاولة للحصول على تأشيرات دخول إلى المملكة المتحدة لحضور اجتماعاتنا.
نود أن نوضح أن الجمعية الطبية العراقية في المملكة المتحدة وأوروبا لا تملك أي وكلاء داخل العراق، كما أننا لم نُفوّض أي شخص أو منظمة بإصدار دعوات نيابةً عنا.
كذلك نود أن نؤكد لجميع الزملاء أن أي نشاط رسمي أو مؤتمر أو فعالية تنظمها هذه الجمعية سيتم الإعلان عنه دائمًا عبر موقعنا الرسمي ومنصات التواصل الاجتماعي الخاصة بنا. كما أن جميع الدعوات ستُرسل مباشرةً من الجمعية إلى الأطباء العراقيين الراغبين بالمشاركة في فعالياتنا وليس عن طريق طرف ثالث.
لقد قامت الجمعية بالفعل بإبلاغ الجهات المعنية بشأن هذه الوثائق المزورة، وطلبت رفض أي طلبات يتم تقديمها باستخدام مثل هذه الدعوات الوهمية.
ننصح بشدة جميع المستلمين بعدم التعامل مع أي دعوة صادرة من أي جهة أخرى، سواء من داخل العراق أو خارجه، ما لم تكن صادرة مباشرةً من الجمعية الطبية العراقية في المملكة المتحدة وأوروبا و معززه بنسخه إلى الجهات المعنيه لمنح تاشيره الدخول إلى المملكه المتحده.
مع الود وفائق الأحترام
